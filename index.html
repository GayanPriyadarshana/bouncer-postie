How to fix the broken ones
  1. Use "Fix found" first. Those sites work under another version of the address — just update your list.
  2. Review "Other domain". Compare the final address and page title. Same organisation (rebrand or new domain)? Use the new URL. Unrelated? The domain was probably sold or hijacked.
  3. Look up Dead, Parked and Placeholder. Open the archive snapshot to see the old site and the organisation's name, then search that name — their LinkedIn or Facebook page usually lists the current website.
  4. Fix the leftovers by hand. What's left is normally a short list for a person to check.

“OK” means the site loads, not that it's the right content. Titles that don't match what you expect are a quick way to spot domains that now belong to someone else.
